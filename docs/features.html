Everything · in one small pill

Every feature.

All of it lives in one bar that floats on top of your screen. Click the gear on the left for settings, or right-click the tray icon.

Monitoring

CPU, RAM, GPU, disk and networkLive rings and numbers, smoothly animated, with the same data sources Task Manager uses.
Top 3 appsClick any metric to see which apps use the most of it, including protected system processes.
Every driveEach disk is tracked on its own; the pill shows the busiest one.
Network adaptersDownload and upload speed of your real adapters, and the busiest ones in the panel.
Card view with graphsDouble-click for a larger card with 60-second graphs for every metric and the top apps underneath.
Hot warningsValues and labels change colour when something runs near its limit.
Refresh rate and smoothingPick how often it samples and how much the numbers are smoothed.
Choose your metricsShow only the ones you care about; the pill resizes to fit.
🌡

Temperatures

GPUFrom the same source as Task Manager, no extra drivers.
NVMe drivesRead straight from the drive.
CPU (AMD Ryzen)Through AMD's Ryzen Master SDK, in admin mode.
SATA drivesFrom the drive's own SMART sensors, in admin mode.
Admin mode, asked onceOne permission prompt, then it starts at login through a scheduled task with no more prompts.
Heat labelsCPU, GPU and disk labels turn red when they get hot.

Recording & transcription

One-tap recordingThe red button (or Ctrl+Alt+R) records everything playing on your PC plus your microphone.
Live transcriptA strip under the pill shows what's being said as it happens; click it to see more lines.
Who said whatVoices are told apart automatically and labelled; your microphone is labelled "You".
Accurate pass after stoppingA second, more careful pass over the whole recording for a cleaner transcript.
SummaryOverview, key points, decisions and action items, written by a local model or by Gemini.
Gemini transcription (optional)Send the finished recording to Gemini with your own key; the audio is deleted from Google afterwards.
Keep the audioSave one listenable file of the whole meeting next to the transcript.
Recordings windowBrowse past recordings with transcript, audio playback and summary in one place, no transcriber needed.
Soft soundsGentle tones when recording starts, stops and is saved.
Fully stops when doneThe transcriber exits when you stop, so it uses nothing in the background.
✦

Explain & search

One tap to turn onTap Peter once; explain mode stays on until you tap him again.
Glowing cursorYour arrow, text and hand cursors glow while it's on, with zero lag.
Tooltip by your highlightHighlight text in any app and a small Explain | Search tooltip appears right there.
ExplainA short, plain answer: definitions for words, what code does, what a sentence means.
SearchOpens a web search for the highlighted text.
Your choice of AIOn your PC (private, free), Claude or Gemini.
Read aloudAnswers can be spoken in Windows' built-in voice, free and offline.
ShortcutCtrl+Alt+E explains whatever is highlighted, even with the mode off.
♫

Music

Works with any playerSpotify, YouTube in a browser, Media Player and anything that shows in Windows' media controls. No sign-in.
Now playing on the barCover art, song, artist and little dancing bars.
Previous, play/pause, nextRight on the pill, no need to open anything.
ProgressA thin line on the bar, plus time played and time left in the player.
Player panelBig cover art, shuffle and all the controls.
VolumeA slider in the player and mouse-wheel over the music; it changes the app's own volume, not the whole PC's.

Placement & hiding

Drag anywhereSnaps to edges, corners and centre, and never leaves the screen.
Multiple monitorsMoves between screens and stays sharp at every scaling level.
Vertical on the sidesDrag it to the left or right edge and it becomes a vertical capsule.
Swipe to tuckThrow it against a screen edge and it slides away, leaving a soft glowing line.
Tap to bring backTap the line and the pill returns and stays.
Living lineThe tucked line glides while music plays and breathes red while recording.
Optional auto-hideTuck automatically when the mouse is away.
Click-through and hideLet clicks pass through it, or hide it completely from the tray.

Look & feel

8 themesGraphite, Midnight, OLED, Glass, Frost, Light, Sunset and Mono.
Size and opacityMake it smaller, bigger or more see-through.
Frame rate15, 25, 30 or 60 fps; higher is smoother and uses a little more CPU.
Reduced animationsFor the lowest possible CPU use.
ClockTime and date in your Windows format.
Hourly momentOn the hour a band of light sweeps across with a soft chime, and a gentler ding at half past.
PeterA small character who blinks, looks around while busy, follows your cursor in explain mode and wears a halo when a cloud AI is on.
Settings in the pillA gear opens a settings panel in the pill's own style; the tray has the same menu.
⬇

AI & models

Separate choicesPick local or cloud for highlights and for transcription independently.
Claude and Gemini keysPaste a key once; it's recognised automatically and stored encrypted for your Windows account only.
Model libraryDownload speech, text and helper models, sorted into categories.
Performance predictionsSee how fast each model will run on your machine right now before choosing it.
GPU when availableLocal summaries use your graphics card if it has room, and the CPU otherwise.
Unloaded after useModels are only in memory while they're working.
⚙

System

Tiny and nativeUnder 1 MB, written in Rust with Direct2D; no browser engine inside.
Light on resourcesAbout 2% of one core even while animating, and nothing extra when idle.
Starts with WindowsOptional, from the settings.
Never steals focusClicking the pill doesn't pull you out of the app you're in.
One copy at a timeStarting it again just shows the running one.
Crash-safeBuilt to recover cleanly; your cursors are restored even after an unexpected exit.
